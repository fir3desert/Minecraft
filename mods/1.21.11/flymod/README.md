# FlyMod

FlyMod is a lightweight utility mod for Minecraft that gives players a quick and simple flight toggle.

## Command

The entire flight feature is controlled by a single command:

- `/fly`

When activated, the player gains flight for a fixed duration of 30 minutes.

## Behavior

While flying, the player can move freely through the air without requiring a normal creative or spectator flight mode.

A unique mechanic of this mod is that breaking blocks while flying does not behave like normal mining at full speed. Instead, block breaking feels as if the player is jumping while in the air, which gives the effect of reduced or altered mining speed during flight.

## Notes

- Single command: `/fly`
- Duration: 30 minutes
- Flight effect: active for a limited time
- Special behavior: block breaking while flying behaves like jumping rather than normal full-speed mining
